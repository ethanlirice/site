Signal discrimination of doubly charged Higgs bosons against same-sign WW
background in ATLAS simulation.

I analyzed **31 kinematic features** across **75,000+ collision events**,
iterating feature sets against univariate AUC and downstream classifier
performance, and trained boosted decision trees to **95% test AUC**.

Presented at the UW Undergraduate Research Symposium.
